# Focused evidence matrix

Focused primary-study review for D2, checked 2026-09-21. Scope: sparse forearm/wrist sEMG, placement and session change, IMU fusion, reproducible acquisition and validation. This is a targeted review of the attached plan's six studies, not a systematic or exhaustive search. Publisher access was inconsistent; primary open-access copies were used where available. Accuracy in these papers is not CoachSim macro-F1 and is not a directly comparable benchmark.

| Study and source | Design and sensing | Relevant evidence | Limit and CoachSim decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kim et al. 2021, Sensors 21:7681, [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8624257/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21227681) | Ten healthy right-handed adults; eight-channel custom forearm armband at 500 Hz; 12 postures arranged into seven alternative groups; repeated donning/doffing over ten experimental sessions | Placement shifts degraded recognition; adding training sessions and selecting distinguishable posture groups helped. MAV examples differed substantially between one and nine training sessions. | Their seven groups are not our seven labels. Repeated experimental sessions do not by themselves establish separate-day generalization. Record placement and hold out entire sessions; keep our classes fixed. |
| Colli Alfaro and Trejos 2022, Sensors 22:1321, [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8963034/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s22041321) | Myo EMG and IMU; seven gestures at four arm positions; 24 recruited, 22 included in classification; user-independent models | Table 2 reports adaptive LS-SVM mean accuracy 83.5% with EMG and 84.6% with fusion; bilinear model 42.8% and 67.5%. Fusion benefit depends strongly on model. | User-independent evaluation differs from our participant-specific cross-session test. Include EMG-only, IMU-only and fusion with identical splits; do not promise a particular gain. |
| Botros, Phinyomark and Scheme 2022, IEEE Access 10:125942–125954, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3225761), [author thesis containing article](https://unbscholar.dspace.lib.unb.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/f4dfa3e2-f5a7-4220-bd05-51d60818a65d/content) | Twelve able-bodied participants; four wrist and four forearm electrodes at 1 kHz; five gestures, eight repetitions, three separate-day sessions. Verified in author thesis chapter 4, Methods 4.3.1. | Static wrist classification accuracy decreased by 3.54 and 8.40 percentage points on the second and third days; adaptive/recalibrated methods were examined separately. | Recalibration uses new-day labels and is not a zero-calibration result. Keep the primary test session untouched, report any later recalibration separately, and record actual day gaps. |
| Tacca et al. 2024, Scientific Reports 14:18564, [full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-64458-x), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-64458-x) | Ten able-bodied participants; sleeve with up to 150 electrodes and 59–75 bipolar channels depending on size; 37 sequential movements and a separate mixed set | Reports 97.3% sequential and 92.8% mixed bin-wise accuracy; real-time-oriented transition evaluation is lower. | High-density hardware and different tasks/splits prevent direct comparison with four channels. Motivate sparse-system tradeoffs and evaluate transitions/latency separately from held postures. |
| Côté-Allard et al. 2019, Sensors 19:2811, [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6631507/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s19122811) | Ten-channel dry-electrode armband at 1000 samples/s, nine-axis IMU; 22 able-bodied participants and 11 hand/wrist gestures, wearing the proposed and Myo devices simultaneously | The primary abstract and dataset section report that the proposed device outperformed Myo across three classifiers/modalities with at least ten seconds training per gesture. Source code, hardware design and dataset were released. | Hardware comparison and training requirements differ from CoachSim. Publish an inspectable hardware/data package; record actual costs rather than transferring this paper's historical estimate to CoachSim. |
| Eby et al. 2025, Scientific Data 12:440, [full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04763-w), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04763-w) | Nineteen individuals, two days, 16 bilateral bipolar channels at 2 kHz; typing task, 27 classes | Dataset includes event alignment, raw and processed data, and within-session, cross-session and cross-person baselines; cross-session/person generalization is harder than within-session. | Typing differs from maintained postures. Adopt event provenance, separate days and transparent file organization, without borrowing typing accuracy as an expected result. |

## Synthesis for the thesis

The defensible contribution is a reproducible low-cost sensing-to-feedback implementation and a small cross-session feasibility experiment, not novelty of EMG gesture recognition. Placement variability, class selection and evaluation design affect results. Fusion must be tested, and higher-density results cannot establish performance of a sparse rig. A technically complete negative result can still explain a useful system limitation.

## Technical source outside the primary-study matrix

[MyoWare 2.0 advanced guide](https://cdn.sparkfun.com/assets/learn_tutorials/1/9/5/6/MyoWare_v2_AdvancedGuide-Updated.pdf) and [SparkFun getting started](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/getting-started-with-the-myoware-20-muscle-sensor-ecosystem/all) distinguish RAW, RECT and ENV outputs. The raw path's approximately 20–500 Hz passband motivates kilohertz-class raw sampling, but its roll-off alone does not prove adequate anti-alias rejection. Use RAW, record the analogue chain, and qualify it experimentally.

## Reference corrections

The attached plan's bracket references are not consistently aligned with its six-item list (including references to an absent [9]). Cite stable author/year or DOI identifiers until final manuscript numbering is generated. The Eby article is Scientific Data 12:440. The high-density sleeve paper is a system comparison, not evidence that four MyoWare channels can match its accuracy.
